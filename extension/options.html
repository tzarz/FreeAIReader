VOICE MODEL

Download and prepare Kokoro

Kokoro is an 82 million parameter neural voice model. Its quantized ONNX weights are about 92 MB under the Apache 2.0 license. The extension stores the weights in this browser’s private model cache and runs them here.

Model card and license

The first download needs internet access. Speech generation uses local WebAssembly in Firefox, and WebGPU when supported by another browser. Browser cleanup can remove cached files; in Firefox Private Browsing, they may be removed when the private session ends.

PDF READER

Choose a PDF from your computer, or right-click a PDF open in your browser and choose Read from the top. Its text is extracted here and sent to the local browser voice engine.
